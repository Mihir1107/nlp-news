# Project report outline

**NewsMind: NLP-Based News Article Classification, Summarization, and Question Answering System**

Target length about 25–30 pages. Take every number from `docs/RESULTS.md` (generated by nb12) or `results/*.json`,
and cite the notebook it came from. Figures in `report/figures/` (from nb12); others can be exported from the
notebooks' stored outputs.

## Abstract (≈200 words)
- Problem: turning a stream of news into topics, short summaries and answers.
- What was built: 12 experiments covering the NLP syllabus, from-scratch TF-IDF / n-gram LM / TextRank, a QA
  chatbot, and a REST API.
- Headline results: best classifier and its accuracy (Table 1); ROUGE (Table 2); QA EM/F1 (Table 3);
  tweet macro-recall (Table 4).
- One-sentence conclusion: simple models are close to transformers on keyword-heavy news, at a fraction of
  the cost; evaluation choices (metric, references, labels) matter as much as the model.

## 2. Background
- Text preprocessing: tokenization, stop words, stemming vs lemmatization.
- Representations: BoW, TF-IDF (formula with smoothed idf), n-gram language models, smoothing, perplexity.
- Word embeddings: Word2Vec (CBOW, skip-gram), GloVe; averaging.
- Sequence models: RNN, vanishing gradients, LSTM gates, BiLSTM.
- Transformers: self-attention, BERT (masked LM), GPT-2 (autoregressive), DistilBERT/DistilBART (distillation),
  sentence transformers.
- Summarization: extractive (Lead-k, TextRank/PageRank) vs abstractive; ROUGE.
- Extractive QA: retriever–reader; SQuAD.
- Evaluation: accuracy, precision, recall, F1, macro vs micro, bootstrap confidence intervals.

## 6. Limitations
- Label noise in AG News caps achievable accuracy; test-set-only bootstrap ignores training-sample variance.
- ROUGE with single, extractive references; DistilBART length cap.
- QA: reader trained on SQuAD 1.1 cannot abstain; threshold tuned on its own evaluation set; 40 questions are
  few and were written by the project's AI assistant.
- NER hand check and summary review done by the assistant, then checked by the student.
- Latency measured on a memory-constrained laptop (numbers vary between runs).
- `en_core_web_sm` is a small model; n-gram LM without backoff.
